### Question 51

Write a notice to recruit a student for Prof. Smith’s research project on campus sports activities. Specify the duties and requirements of the job.

**Answer key / reference answer:**

NOTICE

Professor Smith is looking for a student assistant for a research project on campus sports activities. The project aims to investigate student participation rates and preferences.

The assistant will be responsible for distributing questionnaires, collecting data, and assisting with data analysis using SPSS software. Requirements include strong communication skills, basic statistical knowledge, and a passion for sports.

Interested students should submit their CV and a brief cover letter to Prof. Smith's office (Room 302, Department of Physical Education) by July 15th. Include your student ID and contact information.

Li Ming

**Analysis:**

本范文符合通知的格式，包括标题、正文和署名。第一段简要介绍了研究项目和招聘目的。第二段详细描述了助理的职责和要求，使用了清晰的语言。第三段提供了申请方式和截止日期。语言表达准确流畅，字数控制在100字左右。内容完整，条理清晰，符合评分标准。
