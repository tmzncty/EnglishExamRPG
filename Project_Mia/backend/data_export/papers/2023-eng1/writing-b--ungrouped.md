### Question 52

Write an essay based on the picture below. In your essay, you should 1) describe the picture briefly, 2) interpret the implied meaning, and 3) give your comments.

**Answer key / reference answer:**

In the picture, two teams of dragon boat racers are paddling vigorously through the water, with crowds both on the bridge and along the banks cheering them on. Delighted by this scene, an elderly lady says to her spouse, “It is really wonderful that the dragon boat racing in our village is becoming increasingly popular and exciting!”

The picture demonstrates the public's great interest in traditional Chinese culture and customs. Nowadays, many Chinese people participate in various cultural activities either during special festivals or in daily life, from which they not only derive pleasure but also acquire deeper knowledge about their own history and culture. Moreover, these activities help provide further impetus for rural revitalization. For example, villagers promote intangible cultural heritage through sports events, art performances or craft fairs in an effort to attract visitors from far and wide.

In summary, holding cultural events in the countryside is a commendable and sustainable way both to keep time-honored cultural traditions alive and to facilitate rural development. We, as the younger generation, are also responsible for doing what we can to preserve Chinese culture and give back to our hometown.

**Analysis:**

这篇文章首先描述了图片，随后揭示了图片所蕴含的传统文化复兴的主题，并阐述了这一趋势对乡村振兴的积极意义。文章最后总结观点，呼吁年轻人为保护中国文化和回馈家乡做出贡献。文章结构完整，语言流畅，表达准确，符合考研英语写作的要求。
