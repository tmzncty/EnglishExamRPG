# 2014 English I — Section II Part C

### Passage

Music means different things to different people and sometimes even different things to the same person at different moments of his life. It might be poetic, philosophical, sensual, or mathematical, but in any case it must, in my view, have something to do with the soul of the human being. Hence it is metaphysical; but the means of expression is purely and exclusively physical: sound. I believe it is precisely this permanent coexistence of metaphysical message through physical means that is the strength of music. (46) It is also the reason why when we try to describe music with words, all we can do is articulate our reactions to it, and not grasp music itself.

Beethoven’s importance in music has been principally defined by the revolutionary nature of his compositions. He freed music from hitherto prevailing conventions of harmony and structure. Sometimes I feel in his late works a will to break all signs of continuity. The music is abrupt and seemingly disconnected, as in the last piano sonata. In musical expression, he did not feel restrained by the weight of convention. (47) By all accounts he was a freethinking person, and a courageous one, and I find courage an essential quality for the understanding, let alone the performance, of his works.

This courageous attitude in fact becomes a requirement for the performers of Beethoven’s music. His compositions demand the performer to show courage, for example in the use of dynamics. (48) Beethoven’s habit of increasing the volume with an extreme intensity and then abruptly following it with a sudden soft passage was only rarely used by composers before him.

Beethoven was a deeply political man in the broadest sense of the word. He was not interested in daily politics, but concerned with questions of moral behavior and the larger questions of right and wrong affecting the entire society.

(49) Especially significant was his view of freedom, which, for him, was associated with the rights and responsibilities of the individual: he advocated freedom of thought and of personal expression.

Beethoven’s music tends to move from chaos to order as if order were an imperative of human existence. For him, order does not result from forgetting or ignoring the disorders that plague our existence; order is a necessary development, an improvement that may lead to the Greek ideal of spiritual elevation. It is not by chance that the Funeral March is not the last movement of the Eroica Symphony, but the second, so that suffering does not have the last word. (50) One could interpret much of the work of Beethoven by saying that suffering is inevitable, but the courage to fight it renders life worth living.

### Question 46

It is also the reason why when we try to describe music with words, all we can do is articulate our reactions to it, and not grasp music itself.

**Answer key / reference answer:**

这也解释了为什么当我们试图用语言去描述音乐的时候，充其量只能是说清楚自己的感受，而无法抓住音乐本身。

**Analysis:**

本句的重点在于理解“articulate our reactions”和“grasp music itself”之间的对比。前者是我们可以做到的，后者是我们无法做到的，这种对比体现了音乐的抽象性。翻译时注意“articulate”可以根据语境翻译为“表达”或“说清楚”。

### Question 47

By all accounts he was a freethinking person, and a courageous one, and I find courage an essential quality for the understanding, let alone the performance, of his works.

**Answer key / reference answer:**

总而言之，他是一个思想不受束缚的人，而且是一个富有勇气的人。我认为勇气是理解——更是演奏——其作品不可或缺的品格。

**Analysis:**

本句包含一个固定短语“By all accounts”，意为“总而言之”。后半句的重点在于理解“let alone”的用法，表示“更不用说”。翻译时注意语气的递进关系，即勇气对于理解作品是重要的，但对于演奏作品来说则更为重要。

### Question 48

Beethoven’s habit of increasing the volume with an extreme intensity and then abruptly following it with a sudden soft passage was only rarely used by composers before him.

**Answer key / reference answer:**

贝多芬惯以超凡的力度加大音响，随后突然进入一个意想不到的柔和乐段，这种手法在前人的作品中极为罕见。

**Analysis:**

本句是一个长句，主语是“Beethoven’s habit”，后面是由“increasing…and…following…”构成的动名词短语作后置定语。谓语是“was used”。翻译时可以采用拆分法，将长句拆分成几个短句，使表达更为流畅。

### Question 49

Especially significant was his view of freedom, which, for him, was associated with the rights and responsibilities of the individual: he advocated freedom of thought and of personal expression.

**Answer key / reference answer:**

尤具深意的是他的自由观，在他看来自由关系到个人的权利和责任：他主张思想和个人表达的自由。

**Analysis:**

本句采用倒装结构，将表语“Especially significant”置于句首，以示强调。翻译时需要将其调整为正常语序。后半句是对“freedom”的解释，可以采用顺译法。

### Question 50

One could interpret much of the work of Beethoven by saying that suffering is inevitable, but the courage to fight it renders life worth living.

**Answer key / reference answer:**

苦难是不可避免的，但与之抗争的勇气正是生活的意义所在。对贝多芬的诸多作品，我们都可做此解读。

**Analysis:**

本句的主干是“One could interpret…by saying that…”。“that”引导的是一个宾语从句，其中“suffering is inevitable”和“the courage to fight it renders life worth living”是两个并列的分句。翻译时需要理清句子结构，并注意“render”的用法，表示“使…变得”。
