### Question 52

Write an essay of 160-200 words based on the picture below. In your essay, you should describe the picture briefly, interpret the meaning, and give your comments.

**Answer key / reference answer:**

The cartoon vividly depicts a college student facing a computer screen displaying a course selection system. The courses are divided into two distinct categories: one labeled 'Knowledgeable, Innovative, Challenging,' and the other 'Easy A, Simple Pass, Minimal Homework.' This division reflects a prevalent attitude among some students who prioritize ease and high grades over substantial learning.

The cartoon's message is a critique of a utilitarian approach to education, where students focus on achieving high grades with minimal effort rather than pursuing genuine knowledge and intellectual growth. This trend is often driven by external pressures such as the desire for a high GPA and career prospects. However, such a narrow focus can hinder students' overall development and limit their potential for future success.

In my opinion, this situation calls for a multi-faceted approach. Universities should reform their assessment systems to value genuine learning and critical thinking. Students need to cultivate a passion for knowledge and recognize the long-term benefits of challenging themselves. Parents and society as a whole should promote a culture that values intellectual curiosity and personal growth over mere academic achievements. By shifting our focus from grades to genuine learning, we can empower students to become well-rounded individuals who are prepared to tackle the challenges of the future.
