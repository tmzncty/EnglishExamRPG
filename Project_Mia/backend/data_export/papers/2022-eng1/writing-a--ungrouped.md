### Question 51

Write an email to a professor at a British university, inviting him/her to organize a team for the international innovation contest to be held at your university.

**Answer key / reference answer:**

Dear Professor,

I hope this email finds you well. I am writing to invite you to participate in our International Innovation Contest, which will be held at our university from November 15th to 17th.

The contest aims to foster creative solutions to global challenges. We believe your expertise in [领域] would be invaluable to a team. We encourage you to organize a team of students from your university to join the contest.

We would be honored to have your participation. Please let me know if you are interested by October 20th.

Sincerely,
Li Ming

**Analysis:**

这篇范文结构清晰，首先表达了问候，然后明确了邀请的目的，并具体说明了比赛的主题和时间。接着，强调了教授的专业知识对参赛队伍的重要性，表达了诚挚的邀请。最后，给出了回复的截止日期。全文语言简洁明了，符合邮件的写作规范。
