### Question 51

You are going to host a club reading session. Write an email of about 100 words recommending a book to the club members. You should state reasons for your recommendation.

**Answer key / reference answer:**

Dear Club Members,

I hope this email finds you well. As the host of our upcoming reading session, I'd like to recommend "Sapiens: A Brief History of Humankind" by Yuval Noah Harari. 

This book offers a fascinating and thought-provoking exploration of the history of our species, from the Stone Age to the present day. Harari's writing is engaging and accessible, making complex ideas easy to understand. It provides a broad perspective on human progress and the challenges we face as a global society. I believe it will spark lively discussions and encourage us to think critically about our past, present, and future.

I look forward to seeing you all at the session!

Best regards,
Li Ming

**Analysis:**

该范文结构清晰，语言流畅，完全符合题目要求。首先，开门见山地表明推荐书籍及其作者。其次，用简洁的语言概括了书籍的内容和特点，并阐述了推荐的理由，例如“视角广阔”、“发人深省”、“激发讨论”。最后，表达了对读书会的美好期待。全文逻辑清晰，语言表达准确、地道，书写规范。
