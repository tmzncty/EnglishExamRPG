### Question 52

Directions:

Write an essay of 160-200 words based on the following drawing.In your essay you should
1)describe the drawing briefly,
2)explain its intended meaning,and
3)give your comments.

You should write neatly on the ANSWER SHEET.(20 points)

**Answer key / reference answer:**

The bar chart illustrates the average number of major durable consumer goods owned per hundred households in China in recent years. Generally, there has been a significant increase in the ownership of these goods.

This chart reflects China's economic development and the improvement of people's living standards. With increasing income, families are now able to afford more durable goods, which were once considered luxuries. This trend highlights the shift in consumption patterns and the growing demand for higher quality of life.

From my perspective, while increased consumption can boost the economy, it’s important to maintain a balance. Overconsumption can lead to resource depletion and environmental problems. It's advisable for individuals and families to make rational consumption decisions, prioritizing needs over wants and focusing on sustainable products. Governments and businesses should also promote responsible consumption and environmental awareness to ensure sustainable economic development.

**Analysis:**

本范文结构清晰，包括图表描述、意义解释和个人评论三个部分。第一段概括描述了图表的主要信息，即耐用消费品拥有量的增长。第二段解释了图表所反映的社会经济现象，即经济发展和生活水平提高。第三段提出了个人评论，强调了理性消费和可持续发展的重要性。

写作思路：
1. 仔细观察图表，提取关键信息，如增长趋势、最高和最低拥有量等。
2. 思考图表所反映的社会经济背景，如经济发展、消费升级等。
3. 结合个人观点，对图表所反映的现象进行评论，如理性消费、可持续发展等。

语言方面：
1. 使用准确的词汇描述图表，如'bar chart', 'durable consumer goods', 'average number'等。
2. 使用合适的连接词，使文章逻辑清晰，如'Generally', 'With', 'From my perspective'等。
3. 注意语言的简洁性和流畅性，避免冗余和不必要的重复。
