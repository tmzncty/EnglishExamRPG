### Question 51

Some international students are coming to your university. Write them an email in the name of the Students’ Union to extend your welcome and provide some suggestions for their campus life here. You should write about 100 words on ANSWER SHEET 2. Do not sign your name at the end of the letter. Use “Li Ming” instead. Do not write the address.

**Answer key / reference answer:**

Dear International Students,

On behalf of the Students' Union, I extend a warm welcome to all of you! We are thrilled to have you join our university community.

To help you settle in, here are a few suggestions. Firstly, actively participate in orientation events to familiarize yourselves with the campus and resources. Secondly, don't hesitate to reach out to local students for assistance and friendship. Thirdly, explore the city and its cultural attractions during your free time. Finally, take advantage of language exchange programs to improve your Chinese skills.

We hope you have a wonderful and enriching experience here. If you have any questions or need assistance, please don't hesitate to contact the Students' Union.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

**Analysis:**

这篇范文的写作思路如下：
1.  **开头**: 明确写信目的，代表学生会表达欢迎。
2.  **主体**: 提供3-4条切实可行的建议，例如熟悉校园、结交朋友、探索城市、提升语言能力。每条建议都简洁明了。
3.  **结尾**: 表达祝愿，并告知对方如有问题可联系学生会。
该范文语言表达流畅自然，用词准确，内容充实，结构完整，符合考研英语写作的要求。
