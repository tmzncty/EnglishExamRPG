1) describe the picture briefly,
2) explain its intended meaning, and
3) give your comments.

**Answer key / reference answer:**

The cartoon depicts two students standing in front of a campus bulletin board covered with posters advertising academic lectures. One student, looking dismissive, remarks, “It’s not our major, so it’s not much help listening.” The other student, in contrast, replies optimistically, “It must be beneficial to listen.”

This picture subtly conveys the importance of interdisciplinary learning. In today's rapidly evolving world, confining oneself to a single field of study is increasingly limiting. The cartoon highlights the necessity of venturing beyond one's comfort zone and embracing knowledge from diverse disciplines. By attending lectures outside of our major, we expose ourselves to new perspectives, ideas, and problem-solving approaches that can enrich our understanding and broaden our skill set.

In conclusion, the illustration serves as a potent reminder to cultivate intellectual curiosity and embrace lifelong learning. A willingness to explore beyond the confines of one's major is not merely beneficial but essential for personal and professional growth in an interconnected world. We should adopt an open mind and actively seek opportunities to expand our horizons, thereby positioning ourselves for success in an increasingly complex and competitive landscape.

**Analysis:**

这篇范文首先简要描述了图片内容，点明了两个学生对跨专业讲座的不同态度。接着，文章阐述了图片的寓意，强调了跨学科学习的重要性，并分析了其益处。最后，文章总结了观点，再次强调了培养求知欲和终身学习的重要性。文章结构完整，语言流畅，逻辑清晰，符合题目要求。
