### Question 52

Write an essay of 160-200 words based on the following drawing. In your essay you should describe the drawing briefly, explain its intended meaning, and give your comments.

**Answer key / reference answer:**

As is vividly depicted in the cartoon, a bottle has fallen to the ground, and half of the liquid inside has spilled. Two men react differently to this situation. The man on the left, looking distressed, exclaims, "It's all gone!" while the man on the right, appearing optimistic, says, "Luckily there's still some left!"

**Analysis:**

本篇范文首先描述了图画的内容，然后解释了图画的含义，最后给出了自己的评论。文章结构清晰，语言流畅，表达准确。范文强调了积极心态的重要性，以及在面对困境时应该如何保持乐观。
