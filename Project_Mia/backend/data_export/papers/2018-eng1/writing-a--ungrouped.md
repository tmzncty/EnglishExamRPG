### Question 51

Write an email to all international experts on campus inviting them to attend the graduation ceremony. In your email you should include time, place and other relevant information about the ceremony.

**Answer key / reference answer:**

Dear International Experts,

I hope this email finds you well.

I am writing to invite you to our upcoming graduation ceremony, a significant event celebrating the achievements of our students. The ceremony will be held on June 20th, 2018, at 10:00 AM in the university auditorium. Following the ceremony, there will be a reception in the adjacent hall.

Your presence would be a great honor for our graduates and the entire university community. Your expertise and contributions have undoubtedly enriched their learning experience.

Please RSVP by June 10th, 2018, so we can make the necessary arrangements.

Thank you for your time and consideration. We look forward to seeing you there.

Sincerely,
Li Ming

**Analysis:**

这篇范文结构清晰，语言流畅，内容完整，符合邮件格式。首段点明主旨，说明邀请目的；第二段详细说明时间、地点及重要性；第三段给出回复截止日期，结尾礼貌署名。全文用词正式得体，适合发给专家的邀请函。
